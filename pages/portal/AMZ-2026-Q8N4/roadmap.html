SECTION 01

What this system is for.

A tailored, AI-enabled CRM that matches a boutique, high-end practice. It is the hub for inquiries and marketing. It does not take over project management, contracts, or invoices.

02
of 06

The system captures inquiries, shows the prospect pipeline, holds contacts (including a clean move from Mailchimp and historical spreadsheets), sends elegant follow-ups, and reports clearly. Asana stays the project tool. HoneyBook and DocuSign stay the contract and proposal tools. QuickBooks stays the invoice tool.

Ownership

Single-tenant, on a Hostinger VPS.

Multiple logins and role-based access. Amazáe keeps the code and the data. Hosting stays on a VPS the firm controls.

AI layer

Reminders, not a second salesperson.

Proactive reminders and short insights that fit a detail-oriented team. Summaries and next-step suggestions. No invented metrics.

Recommendation

Build custom. Do not extend Pipedrive.

An exact fit to the inquiry and event workflow, no per-user fee, a native AI layer, and full data ownership. Pipedrive stays an option only if speed matters more than that fit.

Boundary

Sales and marketing only.

Clean hand-off points to Asana, HoneyBook, and QuickBooks. The CRM does not duplicate those records.

SECTION 03

How it is built.

The stack in the roadmap. Chosen for a system Amazáe owns, not for a look we have not designed yet.

04
of 06
Application

Next.js, TypeScript, Tailwind.

App Router. The interface can be styled to Amazáe’s site. PostgreSQL with Prisma. Auth.js with Admin, Sales, and Marketing roles.

Email

Send mail, and keep Mailchimp groups.

Native transactional email, plus a Mailchimp API sync so existing groups are not thrown away.

Connected tools

Calendar, Zoom, website forms.

Google Calendar API, Zoom API, and webhooks from the website form. OpenAI or an equivalent for summaries, reminders, and next-step suggestions.

Hosting

Hostinger VPS.

Ubuntu, Nginx, PM2, Let’s Encrypt, and automated backups. Deployment notes and a maintenance handoff come with the build.

SECTION 04

Three months, in order.

Month 1 is the daily sales workflow. Month 2 connects the other tools. Month 3 adds the AI layer and the cleanup.

05
of 06
Month 1
FoundationImmediate priority

Every inquiry is captured. The team can work in it daily.

  • Multi-user login, roles, and an activity log.
  • Contact and prospect records: name, email, phone, inquiry date, source, referral, event type and date, notes, status, next step, due date.
  • Kanban pipeline with stages the team can rename.
  • Tags for Wedding, Corporate, Social, Destination, plus custom tags, and a full timeline.
  • CSV import for spreadsheets and Mailchimp exports, with field mapping and duplicate detection.
  • Website inquiry form, embedded or by webhook, that creates the contact and the pipeline card.
  • A first dashboard: open inquiries by stage, overdue follow-ups, recent activity.
  • Manual email logging and a simple follow-up task.
Month 2
ConnectIntegrations and reporting

Take the manual steps out of follow-up.

  • Google Calendar, both ways, for consultations and availability.
  • A Zoom link created when a consultation is booked.
  • Email sequences: drips, a check-in when there is no reply, and mail triggered by a stage change.
  • Mailchimp audience sync, or native segmented campaigns, so current groups survive.
  • A pre-consultation form that attaches itself to the contact.
  • Reports the team can change, including year-over-year inquiry volume, conversion, and source.
  • Filters, saved views, and bulk actions.
Month 3
PolishAI, training, hardening

Intelligence that matches how the team already works.

  • Reminders such as an inquiry from five days ago with no reply, and a suggested follow-up.
  • A weekly summary of the metrics, and notes on conversion.
  • A suggested next step from the history of that inquiry and similar ones.
  • Duplicate merge, missing-field alerts, and a pass over the old records.
  • Training, documentation, and changes from how the team actually uses it.
  • Monitoring, backups, and performance work on the production server.
  • Optional webhooks later, if a hand-off to Asana, HoneyBook, or QuickBooks is wanted. Not in the core.
SECTION 05

Custom, or stay on Pipedrive.

The roadmap recommends custom. Pipedrive is still a real choice if the priority is the fastest start and ownership can wait.

06
of 06
Recommended

Custom CRM

Matches the boutique inquiry and event-type workflow. Native AI without another SaaS add-on. No per-user monthly fee. Amazáe owns the data and can extend it. Mailchimp and the forms stay on-brand.

Alternative

Keep Pipedrive

Faster to start. Weaker if the goal is a native AI layer and full ownership. The meeting brief asks which pain in Pipedrive, Mailchimp, or the spreadsheets has to be solved either way.
