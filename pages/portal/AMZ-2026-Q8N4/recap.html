Why a CRMKaila

Pipedrive does not match the data they want.

  • The opening of the call was audio and attendance, and a check on which materials had already been shared.
  • Kaila described interest in CRM reporting, and the difficulty of bending Pipedrive to the structure Amazáe prefers.
  • The need is to track a client from inquiry through contract, tighten the sales process, and run marketing funnels for wedding, social, and corporate.
  • Inquiries arrive on more than one channel. Spreadsheet data needs to sit in one place.
Shown
A working CRMRandy

Custom was described as a fit. A franchise CRM was shown as an example.

  • Consulting was distinguished from a build that Farber would host and maintain.
  • The example had tenant dashboards, customer records, document storage, and AI-supported workflows.
  • The group looked at proactive AI prompts, guardrails that can be configured, a customer portal, and ways to handle proposals and electronic signatures.
  • Also covered: Google Calendar, email campaigns, customizable settings, employee time tracking, and licensing.
How it would land
Build and cutoverNo interruption

The current tools stay up while this is built.

  • Three months, with feedback early and a staged rollout.
  • VPS deployment was described as a week to 10 days, and as something that does not turn off the systems already in use.
  • Refinements can be folded in as the team uses it. Website inquiry forms can create the lead, and can notify the team and send a reply.
  • Sales, marketing, and reporting come first. Internal processes should be reviewed for what is worth automating.
  • Budget was discussed next to current software cost and the rebrand timeline. No number was recorded in these notes.
